Frequently asked questions

Q. Where are my records stored?

Only on your iPhone. They are never sent to the operator’s servers. Deleting the app deletes your records too.

Q. Can I move my records to a new phone?

Moving to a new iPhone with Quick Start, or restoring from an iCloud backup, carries your records along. To keep a spare copy, use Settings > Moving your records > Export to a file. Import from a file brings it back, replacing all the records on the phone at that point.

Q. The night reminder doesn’t come

Check the following:

  • Night reminder is on in the app’s Settings
  • Notifications are allowed in iPhone Settings > Notifications > Discipline ON
  • You haven’t already closed the day (no reminder comes on a night already closed)
  • A Focus such as Do Not Disturb isn’t on

Q. Which day does a close after midnight count for?

Until the time set in Settings > Day ends at (4 AM at first), a close still counts for the day before.

Q. How do I turn off sounds or vibration?

Use Sound effects and Vibration in Settings. In silent mode, only vibration plays.

Q. How do I erase all my records?

Settings > Erase all records. Journals, tasks and goals are all erased, and this can’t be undone.

Q. How do I cancel FOCUS?

In iPhone Settings > your name > Subscriptions. You can also open it from the app’s Settings > Plan > Manage subscription. After you cancel, FOCUS stays until the end of the current period.

Q. How do I get FOCUS back after changing phones or reinstalling?

With the same Apple Account you bought FOCUS with, tap Settings > Restore purchases in the app.

Q. Can I get a refund?

Refunds for App Store purchases are handled by Apple. See Request a refund (Apple Support).
