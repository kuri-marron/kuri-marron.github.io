Discipline ON

Privacy Policy

This policy explains how marron (“the operator”) handles your information in Discipline ON (“the app”), an app for iPhone.

1. Information the operator collects

The operator does not collect personal information such as your name, email address or phone number through the app.

Your goals, tasks, journals, exercise and other records, and the app’s settings, are stored only on your device. They are never sent to the operator’s servers, and the operator cannot see them.

2. Buying FOCUS, the paid plan

Apple processes FOCUS payments through the App Store. The operator never receives your payment details, such as your credit card.

To confirm and restore purchases, the app uses RevenueCat, a service of RevenueCat, Inc. The following is sent to RevenueCat:

  • Purchase records (the product bought, when it was bought or renewed, and the purchase information Apple issues)
  • An ID the app creates automatically, which does not identify you
  • Information such as the OS and app versions, language, region and currency
  • Your IP address, which is sent along with any network connection

Your records (goals, tasks, journals and so on) are never sent to RevenueCat. RevenueCat handles the information it receives under its own privacy policy.

  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy

3. Notifications

The night reminder is scheduled and shown on your device. No information is sent anywhere for it.

You can change notification permission anytime in iPhone Settings > Notifications > Discipline ON.

4. Saving to Photos, sharing and exporting

  • The app saves the DAY image to Photos only when you choose Save Image from Share. For this, it may ask for permission to add to your photos. The app never reads your photos.
  • Images and files you send with Share or Export to a file are handled under the rules of the app or service you choose to send them to.
  • An exported file contains your records as they are. Take care where you keep it and whom you give it to.

5. Ads and tracking

The app has no ads. It does not track you across other companies’ apps or websites.

6. Sharing with third parties

Since the operator does not collect personal information, it gives none to third parties, apart from the information sent to RevenueCat as described in section 2.

7. Deleting your records

You can delete the app’s records in either of these ways:

  • Tap Erase all records in Settings (journals, tasks and goals are all erased; this can’t be undone)
  • Delete the app from your iPhone

To have your purchase records deleted from RevenueCat, contact the operator at the address below.

8. Changes to this policy

The operator may change this policy when needed. Changes take effect when they are posted on this page.
