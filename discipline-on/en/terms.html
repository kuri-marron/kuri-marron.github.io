1. About the app

The app helps you set a goal, break it down into what to do this week and today, and close each day at night with a journal.

The basic features are free. Buying FOCUS, the paid plan, also opens:

  • Design settings (accent colors, themes and sounds)
  • Looking back (journals and tasks, day by day and week by week)
  • No limit on goals (one goal on the free plan)

2. FOCUS, the paid plan

FOCUS comes in three forms. The price is the amount shown on the app’s purchase screen and on the App Store.

  • Yearly: renews automatically every year.
  • Monthly: renews automatically every month.
  • Lifetime: one payment, yours for as long as the app is provided.

For the yearly and monthly plans:

  • Payment is charged to your Apple Account when you confirm the purchase.
  • The plan renews automatically unless you cancel at least 24 hours before the current period ends. The next period is charged within the 24 hours before the current one ends.

After changing phones or reinstalling the app, tap Restore purchases with the same Apple Account to get FOCUS back.
